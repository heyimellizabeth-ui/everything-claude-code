Know-How

Best Kept Secret

Every year since 2013, BKS creates a free, loving, experimental festival on the second weekend of June — surrounded by beautiful nature at Beekse Bergen.

The Stages
ONE TWO Mainstages — headliners
The Casbah The Secret The Floor Not Nothing The Shore

The Casbah & The Secret for discovery. The Floor, Not Nothing, The Shore for dancing.

You are part of ~300

One of the biggest volunteer teams at the entire festival. Without us, BKS could not exist.

The volunteer office is your home base — hang out, relax, sign in and out. Sara and her assistants are always there.

Thursday 21:30 — Volunteer Meeting. Arrive on Thursday to meet the Friendly Fire team, connect with fellow volunteers, pick up your t-shirt, and sign your contract. Strongly recommended — you'll start the weekend organised and relaxed.

Arriving

Getting There

Plan your travel carefully — transport options change by day. Add extra time for your first shift.

Arrive 1 hour early for your first shift. The crew campsite is a 30–45 min walk from the volunteer office, and travel from Tilburg station takes time. Budget accordingly.

Public Transport — By Day

Thursday
Train → Tilburg station
Take Bus 142 or 143 to Hilvarenbeek, Safaripark. OV-fiets recommended — having a bike at the festival is very convenient. Flyover usually open (7 min walk to crew accreditation).
Friday
Shuttle bus from Tilburg station — €14 return
Runs 09:00–20:00 to the festival entrance (Entrance South). From there, take a second shuttle to Entrance North (reception Beekse Bergen), then short walk to backstage accreditation. Queues can be long — allow plenty of time.
Saturday · Sunday · Monday
No public bus from Tilburg
The bus does not run on Fri/Sat/Sun/Mon. Plan car, bike, or lift. Do not rely on public transport after Thursday.

By Car

Navigation

Navigate to Speelland Beekse Bergen, 5081 NJ Hilvarenbeek.

Follow the yellow signs: Leveranciers — Backstage — Artists. They lead straight to backstage accreditation.

Parking & Thursday Drive-in

Free crew parking at the backstage crew parking area.

On Thursday you can drive up to the crew campsite to drop off gear — until 20:00. Not possible on other days. Fill out the form for your parking ticket.

Without a parking ticket you cannot use crew parking. Pick it up at backstage accreditation.

Accreditation

You'll receive an e-voucher by email in the week of the festival. Check your spam folder.

What you need

✦ E-voucher (printed or on your phone)

✦ Passport or identity card

Where to go

Go to backstage accreditation at Speelland Beekse Bergen, 5081 NJ.

Your Weekend

Volunteer Shifts

You will work a minimum of 18 hours total, distributed across multiple days so you still have plenty of time to enjoy the festival.

Minimum hours

18h

Distributed across days. Sometimes a little more, never leaving you without festival time.

First shift timing

Arrive at the volunteer office 30 min early to sign your contract and get your crew t-shirt.

Setting up your tent first? The campsite is a 45 min walk — plan accordingly.

Subsequent shifts

Be at your task meeting point 10–20 min early. Not sure where that is? Check the Tasks section below or visit the volunteer office.

Security & Artist Hospitality
Come to the volunteer office before your shift to collect a special pass for backstage areas. Return it after your shift.
After your shift

Never leave before your coordinator gives permission. Always ask first.

Then come to the volunteer office to sign out — Sara will be waiting with a beer or soft drink. 🎉

Collective Care: You play a role in festival safety. If a visitor is in distress: ask "Are you ok?", take them away from the crowd to a quiet space, and let your manager with a porto contact the safety department.

✦ Your own tent (or rent/borrow)

Wellbeing

Health, Safety & Sustainability

Personal safety is a shared responsibility. You play an active role in keeping the festival safe and welcoming for everyone.

Incident Response

If you see someone in need:

1. Make them comfortable — take them away from the crowd
2. Don't ask detailed questions
3. Ask for help from a safety guard or a crew member with a transceiver

The safety coordinator analyses the situation and takes the necessary measures.

Confidentiality Advisers

An external confidential adviser is backstage Friday to Sunday. Their office is backstage — if the door is closed they're in conversation, send a message instead.

They are not tied to the festival organisation. Speak to them confidentially about anything that made you uncomfortable.

Name and number will be added closer to the festival.

Disabilities

Disabled visitors enter via the Safari Park entrance. Special parking is reserved there.

Since 2022, the festival grounds are mostly wheelchair-accessible. Disabled decks at stages ONE and TWO — only Mi-VIP pass holders + one companion.

Sustainability

BKS is set in a beautiful forest at Beekse Bergen. We aim for minimal environmental impact:

✦ Bring a reusable water bottle
✦ Pick up trash on the grounds
✦ Dispose of cigarette butts properly
✦ Leave the campsite tidy before departure

Respect & Empathy: Thousands of people from all walks of life attend BKS. Be mindful of space, approach people kindly, and communicate clearly. If you witness harassment or discrimination, bring it to the volunteer office or any crew member.

Rules of Engagement

What We Expect

Breaching these rules can result in being blacklisted and your wristband being cut or blocked.

Hours & Shifts
  • Minimum 18 hours total during the weekend
  • Show up at least 15 minutes before your shift
  • Call or speak to your coordinator in person for cancellations within 48 hours
  • Give immediate notice for emergencies
Clothes
  • Wear your CREW t-shirt during all shifts
  • Do not wear your CREW t-shirt when off duty
Valuables
  • Your valuables are your own responsibility
  • Workers' insurance covers personal injury — not belongings
Drugs & Alcohol — Zero Tolerance on Shift
  • Strictly forbidden to use drugs or arrive drunk for a shift
  • No alcohol or drugs during your shift
  • Fighting and threatening behaviour is not accepted
Artists & Press
  • No SLR cameras or professional photo equipment (pocket cameras & phones OK)
  • No photos of artists, no autograph requests, no contact with artists unless your role requires it
  • Do not speak to the press or on behalf of BKS without authorisation — direct all press questions to the Press Zone backstage
