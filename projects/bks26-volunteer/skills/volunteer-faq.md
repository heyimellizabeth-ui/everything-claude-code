## Minimum Hours Rule

Every volunteer must complete a **minimum of 18 hours total** across all shifts. The coordinator tracks cumulative hours. If a volunteer is at risk of falling short, flag it early — do not wait until Monday.

---

## Sign-In / Sign-Out Procedure

**First shift of the festival:**
- Arrive at the **volunteer office** 30 minutes before your shift start time.
- The volunteer office is the sign-in point for the first shift.

**All subsequent shifts:**
- Arrive at your **task's designated meeting point** 10–20 minutes before shift start.
- Most tasks: meeting point is the **volunteer office**.
- Exception — campsite tasks have their own specific meeting points (see task-briefing skill).

**After every shift:**
- Return to the **volunteer office** to sign out.
- Failure to sign out means the shift may not be counted toward your 18-hour minimum.

## Rules Every Volunteer Must Follow

1. **No drugs or alcohol during shifts.** Zero tolerance — a volunteer found in violation will be removed from the schedule.
2. **Do not wear your crew T-shirt off-duty.** The crew T-shirt identifies you as staff. Off-duty, you are a festival guest; wearing the shirt creates confusion and liability.
3. **No SLR cameras on-site.** Personal SLR/DSLR cameras are not permitted. Smartphone cameras are fine.
4. **No talking to press.** If approached by press or media, decline politely and direct them to the festival's communications team.
5. **No photographing artists.** Do not take photos of artists backstage, in hospitality, or without explicit consent.
6. **Valuables are your own responsibility.** The festival does not cover loss or theft of personal items.
7. **Cancellation policy.** If you need to cancel a shift or your entire participation, contact Sara Verdegaal at least 48 hours in advance. Late cancellations without notice affect the whole team.
