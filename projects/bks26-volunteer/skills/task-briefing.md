# BKS26 Task Briefing

<!-- C1: Task knowledge baked in as a skill. The coordinator can brief any of the 18 task types without an external system or manual lookup. -->

## When to Use This Skill

Activate when a volunteer asks:
- "What do I actually do in my task?"
- "Where is my meeting point?"
- "What should I wear?"
- "What do I need to know before my first shift on this task?"

**Model guidance (C3):** Use **Haiku** for standard task lookups (single task, no conflicts). Escalate to **Sonnet** if the volunteer has a scheduling conflict or is being reassigned across tasks.

---

## Universal Briefing Rules (all tasks)

- Wear your **crew T-shirt** during every shift.
- Carry your **volunteer accreditation** at all times on-site.
- **First shift**: report to the volunteer office 30 minutes early.
- **Subsequent shifts**: report to your task meeting point 10–20 minutes early.
- **After every shift**: sign out at the volunteer office.
- No drugs or alcohol during shifts.
- No SLR cameras. No press contact. No artist photography.

## Example Prompts

```
"I'm on Flexteam — where do I report?"
→ Report to the volunteer office. Flexteam meeting point is always the volunteer office. Be prepared to be deployed to any task area.

"What is the meeting point for Campsite Management - Check-in?"
→ The campsite check-in desk — this is a campsite-specific location, NOT the volunteer office. The exact location will be shown on your arrival briefing map.

"I'm on Runnerteam Horeca — do I need anything special?"
→ Yes. A valid driving licence is required and must be verified before your first shift. Contact Lester & Giulia on +31 85 115 5226 for task-specific questions.

"What does the Ecoteam actually do?"
→ Waste sorting, recycling enforcement, and litter picking in assigned zones. Gloves and high-vis vest are provided. You'll also educate guests on correct bin use.
```
