Getting There

Pick your transport below — each one notes what changes per day. Address: Speelland Beekse Bergen, 5081 NJ Hilvarenbeek.

⚠
No public bus on Sat, Sun & Mon. The regular bus (142/143) only runs Thu & Fri. For the rest of the weekend, plan to bike or drive.
🚲
Bring a bike if you can. The crew campsite is a ~45-min walk from the volunteer office — a bike cuts it to under 10. Rent an OV-fiets at Tilburg station.

Train to Tilburg Centraal, then Bus 142 or 143 to stop Hilvarenbeek Safaripark.

~7-min walk to crew accreditation via the flyover bridge.

Sat–Mon: this bus does not run — use the Friday shuttle window, bike, or drive.

Drops at Entrance South → change to the shuttle for Entrance North (Beekse Bergen reception) → short walk to backstage accreditation.

⚠ Queues can be long on Friday morning — allow extra time.

Step 4

Your Shifts

At least 18 hours total, spread across days. These rules apply to every shift — know them before your first day.

⏰
First shift: Arrive at the volunteer office 30 minutes early to sign your contract and collect your t-shirt (if not done at the Thursday meeting).
All subsequent shifts: Arrive 10–20 minutes early at the task meeting point (see Tasks section).
🔐 Security & Artist Hospitality — Extra Step

If your shift is Security or Artist Hospitality: come to the volunteer office first to collect your special backstage pass before going to the meeting point. Return the pass after your shift ends.

During your shift
  • Never leave your shift before the coordinator gives you explicit permission
  • Wear your crew t-shirt during shifts only — take it off when off-duty
  • No alcohol or drugs during shifts — this includes showing up intoxicated
After every shift
  • Go to the volunteer office to sign out
  • Sara has a beer or soft drink waiting for you
  • Security / Artist Hospitality: return your backstage pass before leaving
Can't make your shift?

Contact your coordinator as soon as possible. If it's less than 48 hours before your shift, call or speak to them in person. No-shows or late cancellations without notice risk wristband blacklisting.

Campsite note

The crew campsite is ~45 minutes walk from the volunteer office (new location this year). Plan your commute time — a bike is strongly recommended.

Section 7

Crew Campsite

The campsite has moved this year — it's now closer to the regular entrance but further from the volunteer office.

📍
New location this year. ~45 min walk from the volunteer office. A bike is strongly recommended for the campsite ↔ backstage commute.
Key facts
  • Opens Thursday 11 June at 16:00
  • Bring your own tent + sleeping gear — nothing is provided
  • Leave luggage at the volunteer office if you arrive before 16:00 Thursday
  • Thursday: bring your own food (unless you have a Thursday shift)
  • Sunday: crew party after last Sunday shifts
Accommodation alternatives

ComfortCamp, Secret Fields glamping, or Beekse Bergen cottages available on the BKS accommodation page — these sell out fast. Camper/caravan spots are limited; apply via form before June 4th.

Section 9

Rules of Engagement

Read these before your first shift. These are not optional — breaking them has consequences.

Never do
  • Drink alcohol or use drugs during shifts — showing up drunk/high = wristband cut
  • Leave your shift before the coordinator gives permission
  • Wear the crew t-shirt off-shift
  • No-show or cancel late without notice — risk of wristband blacklist
  • Fight, threaten or intimidate anyone
  • Photograph artists or approach them for autographs
  • Speak to press on behalf of BKS (refer them to the Press Zone backstage)
  • Bring SLR cameras or professional photo gear
  • Smoke inside backstage tents — outdoor air only
Always do
  • Arrive 10–20 min early at meeting point (30 min early for first shift at volunteer office)
  • Wear crew t-shirt during every shift
  • Sign out at the volunteer office after every shift
  • Contact coordinator ASAP if you can't make a shift (<48h = call or in person)
  • Bring your own reusable water bottle
  • Pick up litter and dispose of cigarette butts properly
  • Look out for guests and colleagues in distress — get help from crew/security
Commitment
  • Minimum 18 hours total, distributed across days
  • Pocket cameras and phones are fine for personal photos
  • Artist privacy: no photos of artists, no press contact
  • Valuables: your responsibility (workers insurance covers personal injury only, not belongings)
Special passes
  • Security & Artist Hospitality: collect backstage pass at volunteer office before shift
  • Return your backstage pass immediately after your shift ends
  • Security gear required Thursday backstage — site is still under construction
Section 10

Health & Safety

Your wellbeing matters. Know who to contact and what support is available.

Feeling ill or unsafe?

Contact Sara: +31 6 40 96 61 09, speak to your task coordinator on-shift, or come directly to the volunteer office.

Confidential adviser

Available backstage Friday through Sunday. Name and contact number will be shared closer to the festival. Completely confidential.

Accessibility
  • Safari Park entrance has special parking for disabilities
  • Stages ONE and TWO have disabled decks
  • Grounds are wheelchair accessible
Respect & empathy

If you see someone being harassed, in distress or in danger — don't ignore it. Get help from nearby crew or security. You're part of making BKS a safe space.

Sustainability
  • Bring your own reusable water bottle
  • Pick up litter when you see it
  • No cigarette butts on the ground
