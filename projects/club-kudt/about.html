03 — Timeline
Dec 2022

Vol. 01 — The First Edition

Club KUDT launches at Podium Victorie, Alkmaar. Noord-Holland's first dedicated queer night is born. Sold out. The dancefloor didn't stop until 02:00.

2023

Vol. 02 & 03 — Finding the Rhythm

Two more editions. The community grows. Drag lineups expand. The Kleine Zaal at Podium Victorie becomes home.

Sep 2024

Museumnacht Alkmaar — Rainbow Paradise

First major collaboration. Club KUDT brings queer energy to Alkmaar's Museum Night — an annual highlight ever since.

2025

Vol. 06 — Growing the Movement

780+ photos archived. A loyal community. Bigger lineups, deeper roots in Noord-Holland's queer scene.

May 2026

Pride After Party — Grolsch Zaal

Biggest edition yet. Moving to the Grolsch Zaal for our Pride Special. Drag, disco, and the whole community.
