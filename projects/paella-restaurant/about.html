Our Story

Fire, salt & patience.

A family kitchen that never learned to hurry.

The Beginning

From a single pan on the sand.

It started with one wide steel pan and a driftwood fire on the beach, cooking for friends as the tide went out. The recipe never changed — only the number of chairs around it. Today we cook the same way: open flame, day-boat catch, and rice that's earned its socarrat.

How We Cook

Nothing arrives frozen.

Our fishmonger knocks each morning; what doesn't come from the boats comes from the market down the road. Saffron from La Mancha, paprika smoked over oak, olive oil pressed this season. We toast, we wait, we listen for the crackle — then we serve.

What We Believe

A few plain rules

Seasonal, always

If it isn't good today, it isn't on the board today. The menu bends to the catch.

Share the table

Paella is meant for the middle of the table, spoons reaching in. Eat like family.

No rushing

Good rice can't be hurried, and neither should dinner. Stay for the second jug.
