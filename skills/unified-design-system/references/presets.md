# Style presets

Pick one per project. Each preset is a starting point; the project's
MASTER.md overrides it.

## soft

Premium, agency feel. Generous whitespace, soft layered shadows, large radii
(16 to 24px), one warm accent, serif or humanist display type with a clean
sans for body. Motion: gentle fades and lifts. Dials around 7/6/3.
Avoid: hard black borders, neon, dense tables.

## minimal

Editorial and calm. Warm off-white or near-black monochrome, strong type
contrast (large display versus small body), flat surfaces, bento grids with
thin rules, muted pastel accents at most. No gradients, no heavy shadows.
Dials around 5/3/3.

## brutalist

Raw and mechanical. Rigid visible grid, extreme type scale jumps, monospace or
grotesk type, high contrast, square corners, utilitarian labels. Motion is
instant or absent. Dials around 8/2/6.
Avoid: soft shadows, rounded pills, pastel palettes.

## product-tool

For dashboards, admin and daily-use apps. Quiet neutral surfaces, one brand
accent for primary actions, compact spacing scale, tabular numbers, clear
states (hover, focus, disabled, loading, empty, error). Dials around 3/2/7.

## trust-first

Public sector, health, finance, kids. Plain language, large readable body
text (18px+), strong contrast, obvious links and buttons, minimal motion.
Prefer an official system (GOV.UK, USWDS) when the brief matches.
Dials around 3/2/5.
