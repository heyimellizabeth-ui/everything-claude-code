## Production Notes

### Intro (0:00–0:16)
Single clean electric guitar, Dm arpeggio, no reverb. Dry and intimate — listener leans in.

### Verse 1 (0:16–0:48)
Drums enter bar 3 with brushes. Bass mirrors the guitar root. Vocals close-mic'd, minimal processing. Everything restrained — tension before release.

### Pre-Chorus (0:48–1:04)
Snare fills on every fourth beat. Bass becomes melodic, active. Guitars build with a hint of overdrive on the top string. Sense of gathering momentum.

### Chorus (1:04–1:36)
Full band arrives. Kick/snare/hi-hat locked in tight. Two guitars: chunky rhythm on the left, tremolo-picking lead on the right. Bass drives the roots hard. Vocals up in the mix. This is where the song opens up.

### Verse 2 (1:36–2:08)
Strip back to half-time drum feel (kick on 1 and 3 only). Reverb increases on vocals. The contrast with the chorus makes re-entry feel earned.

### Bridge (2:44–3:16)
Begins with bass and vocals only — completely bare. Drums re-enter subtly at "What does alive even mean." At "It means—", full band crashes in with distorted guitars, cymbal wash, bass sustain. Hold the final C chord for 4 bars before the chorus returns.

### Final Chorus (3:16–3:44)
Both choruses played through. Add female/high-harmony vocal on the second pass (an octave up on "felt alive"). Room for a guitar solo fill between the two choruses (2 bars, bluesy, minimal).

### Outro (3:44–4:10)
Band drops to clean guitar only. Vocals treated with heavy reverb and slight delay — sounds distant, like memory. Fade over 16 bars. End on Dm.
