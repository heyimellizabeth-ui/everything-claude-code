The idea

No stock art.
No images.
Just code, drawing itself.

Every pixel on this page is generated at runtime from a flow field — a few sine waves summed into motion. The design language (type, color, rhythm) came straight out of the ui-ux-pro-max engine: Exaggerated Minimalism, Syncopate over Space Mono, one electric pink against the dark.

How it reacts

  • 01Move your cursor — particles bend toward it.
  • 02Scroll — the field speeds up and the swirl tightens.
  • 03Prefer calm? Motion off swaps in a static poster frame.
