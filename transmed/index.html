Peer-reviewed sources only

Every claim on this site cites WPATH SOC8, the Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guideline, UCSF Transgender Care, PubMed-indexed studies, or ARUP Consult. No forums, no Reddit, no anecdote — ever.

Clinical content is factual and specific: actual thresholds, actual mechanisms, actual targets. Not "talk to your doctor about everything" — but also not a replacement for your prescribing clinician.
